Privacy Policy

Your trust matters. Here's how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

When you make a booking, we may collect personal details such as your name, contact information, and event details. This information is required to confirm and deliver our services.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for communication, booking confirmations, and service delivery. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

Media Usage

With your consent, selected images and videos from your event may be used for promotional purposes, including on our website, portfolio, and social media platforms. If you prefer your content to remain private, you may request this in writing before your event.

Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, or deletion of, the personal information we hold about you. To make such requests, please contact us directly.

Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with the updated date.
